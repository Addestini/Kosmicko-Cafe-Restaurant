Our story

A garden you can sit inside, built by hand

Tucked inside the Orange Square Building at Phooti Kothi Circle, Birds & Bees is a green pocket of calm. The space is built mostly from upcycled materials and handmade decor, and the kitchen keeps things fresh, simple and fully vegetarian.

🌿

Green everywhere

Ferns, creepers and potted plants on every wall and ceiling.

🪴

Upcycled & handmade

Most of what you sit on and look at has been reused or made by hand.

🥗

Pure veg kitchen

Every single item on the menu is vegetarian.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Is everything vegetarian?

Yes. Every item on the menu is 100% pure vegetarian.

What are your hours?

We're open every day, 10:30 AM to 11:00 PM.

Where exactly are you?

Phooti Kothi Circle, Scheme No. 71, Gumasta Nagar, Indore 452009, near Brand Factory. Look for the wall of green.

How much does a meal cost?

About ₹200–400 per person. Pizzas start at ₹150, sandwiches at ₹100 and mojitos at ₹130.

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome. We recommend booking for evenings, weekends and groups.

How do I order at my table?

Tick your items, choose your table (1 to 10) and place the order. It comes to us on WhatsApp, and you pay at the counter.

Can I order food home?

Yes, through Zomato delivery.
